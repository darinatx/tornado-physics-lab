# Code of Conduct

Tornado Physics Lab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized content, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Remember that tornadoes hurt real people. Keep discussion of real events and their victims
  respectful.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer (GitHub user
`darinatx`), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or PR. Reports are
kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may
block anyone who keeps breaking it, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
